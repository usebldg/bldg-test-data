**Changes.** One floor of each site was selected. The images are our own renders of its geometry (doors drawn as
outlined rectangles in the wall: the source has no door swing). The ground truth is that geometry converted to
floorplan/v0 in image pixels. Each `*.orig.csv` keeps the source rows that were used.
